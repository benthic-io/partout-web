## Android — invite-only testing

PartOut ships to testers through Google's Firebase App Distribution. If you
have an Android phone (Android 8 or newer):

1. Email **[brian@benthic.io](mailto:brian@benthic.io)** with the Google
   account you use on your phone.
2. You get an invitation link; install the build. Testers see new builds the
   same way.

Sign in with the same Google account, or with email and password — your cars,
verdict history, and uploads live behind that account on our servers.

## What you do with it

Add a car (VIN scan or configuration picker), then swipe the candidate parts
you can verify under the hood. Each session takes minutes and permanently
improves the public fitment database.

## Web version

The app will run in your browser at **[/app](/app/)** — sign-in and the full
swipe deck included. That surface is under construction; the link activates
when it ships.

## iOS

Not yet. Needs a Mac and a signing decision; when it's real it will be
announced here.

## Why testers matter

Every swipe is a first-hand observation of a real car. Two independent owners
agreeing is what turns a candidate part number into published fact. The
earlier the crowd, the faster your search for that one alternator gets
answers.
