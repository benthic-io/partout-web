**partout** /pä(r)ˈto͝o/ *adv.* — everywhere; in every corner: said of parts.

**PartOut** /pä(r)ˈto͝o dŏt prō/ *web URL*: the crowd-sourced database of which
parts actually fit your exact vehicle — built by the people who pull them off
cars.

## What it is

Every car of the same year, make, and model quietly differs: trim, drivetrain,
doors. Factory fitment data says what *should* be there; only hands in the
engine bay say what *is* there. PartOut turns thousands of those first-hand
observations into a public, configuration-keyed parts database anyone can
build on.

- **You add your car** — VIN or configuration — and PartOut assembles the
  candidate parts for its exact configuration.
- **You swipe what you see**: right for *present and resaleable*, left for
  *absent or not resaleable*. Thirty seconds, no expertise required.
- **Nothing publishes on one voice.** Facts come from independent agreement:
  the human majority is decisive, labelled agent fallbacks only fill gaps —
  they never override people — and every route to publication says how it
  got there.

## Why it exists

PartOut is a crowd-sourced **public data engine**, not a data broker. We do
not sell your data. Your interactions build a shared parts commons that the
whole repair ecosystem — yards, restorers, parts finders — can rely on. The
catalog is keyed by **vehicle configuration, never by VIN**: your car's
identity stays yours.
